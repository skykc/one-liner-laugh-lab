One funny line answering the prompt — the excuse a robot vacuum gives after
eating a sock:

> "I didn't eat your sock. I adopted it. It lives under the couch now and
> pays rent in lint."

The joke is one concrete image (the sock under the couch) plus one twist
(adoption, not theft — with rent in lint). 89 characters, one line.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, where the house gate
(1–200 characters of UTF-8 text) and the pairwise judge read it. Nothing
is generated at run time, so the judged text is exactly the reviewed line.
